[Apache-2.0](LICENSE). That grant permits commercial deployment, modification,
redistribution and paid hosting; a separate project license is not required.

Implementation, managed hosting, maintenance, training and support may be offered
under separate service agreements. Those agreements define pricing, support,
warranties and deployment responsibilities; the open-source grant itself creates
no service or SLA commitment. Existing public security, governance, audit and
recovery features remain covered by Apache-2.0.

This repository includes no separately licensed commercial edition. Any future
separate extension must identify its own distribution and terms; it cannot change
the existing community grant or automatically acquire extra rights to contributors'
work. Contributors retain their copyrights.

Dependencies, datasets, models and hosted providers keep their own terms.
Historical releases keep their original grants. See the [component license index](LICENSES.md)
