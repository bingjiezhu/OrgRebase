# Code of conduct

Participants should be respectful, precise and honest about evidence. Technical
disagreement is welcome when it concerns behavior, contracts and reproducible
results. Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and disclosure of
another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive, unsafe or privacy-violating contributions and
restrict participation. To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the
channel accompanying your source delivery and request a private conversation.
Avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be assessed in context,
with the privacy of everyone involved protected as far as possible.

This policy does not create a guaranteed support or response-time commitment.
Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).
