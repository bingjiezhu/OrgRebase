# Governance

OAC is currently a maintainer-led experimental proposed draft. It is not an accredited standard and
has no formal certification program.

Normative decisions require:

1. a public problem statement and alternatives;
2. an explicit compatibility and migration analysis;
3. machine-readable fixtures demonstrating the intended semantics;
4. disclosure of conflicts, rejected evidence, and unresolved questions;
5. a recorded decision with effective version.

The reference compiler has no special authority over the specification. A disagreement between prose,
schema, and TCK is a specification defect until the owning version resolves it; implementations must
not choose the most permissive interpretation.
