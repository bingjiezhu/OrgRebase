# Licensing

OAC deliberately separates normative prose from executable interoperability assets.

| Paths | License |
|---|---|
| `standard/`, `docs/`, `specs/`, `ctk/protocol/`, Markdown prose under `profiles/`, root project prose (`README*`, `AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `NOTICE.md`, `PATENT-NON-ASSERTION.md`) | CC BY 4.0 |
| `src/`, `schemas/`, `tck/` except `ctk/protocol/`, `implementations/`, `tests/`, `scripts/`, machine-readable examples and fixtures, build/CI/editor metadata, `CITATION.cff`, `THIRD_PARTY.yml` | Apache License 2.0 |

The full license texts are in `LICENSES/`. Dataset material keeps its upstream license and is
listed in `THIRD_PARTY.yml`. This repository is an experimental proposed draft, not a claim of
recognition by a standards body.

Files under `LICENSES/` are the verbatim license texts identified by their filenames. Upstream dataset
material, if ever added, remains under its upstream terms regardless of its local path and must be
declared in `THIRD_PARTY.yml`.
