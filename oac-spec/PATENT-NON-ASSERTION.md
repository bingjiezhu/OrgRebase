# Draft OAC Patent Non-Assertion

**Status: project draft requiring independent legal review before any standards-adoption claim.**

Each maintainer publishing a normative OAC release states an intention not to assert patent claims it
controls that are necessarily infringed solely by a conforming implementation of that release, against
an implementer for making, using, offering, or distributing that conforming implementation.

This statement does not cover optional features, implementation techniques not required by the
normative specification, third-party claims, trademarks, or claims acquired for assertion. The
commitment may terminate defensively against a party that first asserts a patent claim against an OAC
implementer or contributor concerning OAC implementation.

No contributor may represent this draft as legal advice, an OSI license term, a standards-body patent
policy, or evidence that all essential claims have been identified. A future governance decision may
replace this document with a recognized standards patent policy; release records must identify which
text governed each normative version.
