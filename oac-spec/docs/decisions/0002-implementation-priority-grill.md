# ADR 0002: Hard Grill implementation priorities

**Status**: Accepted  
**Date**: 2026-08-23  
**Decision authority**: user-authorized self-questioning; each answer below is the recommended answer

## Why this decision exists

The prior portfolio contains many ambitious Specs. Implementing them in document order would create a
large framework before proving the central idea. This decision selects the shortest sequence that can
falsify OAC's distinctive thesis and records why attractive alternatives were deferred.

## Hard self-questioning

### What must be implemented first?

**Answer: the compiler-independent acceptance boundary, then the reference compiler.** A generated team is only
a proposal. Without a compiler-independent definition of acceptable plans, “dynamic” means arbitrary
and the product collapses into another orchestration library. The models and Profile constraints are
therefore frozen before compiler strategy, and the verifier cannot import compiler code.

### What is the smallest end-to-end proof?

**Answer:** two frozen source roots produce one derived obligation set; two different plans satisfy the
same set; targeted corruptions are rejected with stable reasons; identical reruns are byte-repeatable.
This proves constraint-defined plurality without requiring a runtime or an LLM.

### Does a deterministic compiler contradict dynamic architecture?

**Answer: no.** Dynamic means the organization is a function of admitted facts and a demand, rather
than a hard-coded team. Determinism makes that function auditable. Future search-based, learned, or
federated compilers may choose different witnesses under the same acceptance contract.

### Should an LLM generate the MVP plan?

**Answer: no.** It would add nondeterminism without testing the standard's unique property. LLM-based
compilers become useful only after a compiler-independent verifier can reject unsupported invention.

### Are ten public-data-backed cases enough?

**Answer: enough for engineering feasibility, not enough for a research or enterprise claim.** They may
exercise schemas, counterfactuals, mutations, provenance, and annotation workflow. They remain
`exploratory` until the declared independent-human review gate is met.

### What does “minimal sufficient” mean in this version?

**Answer:** no selected role binding may be removed while retaining mandatory obligation coverage,
qualification, separation, ordering, evidence, and Unknown constraints under the frozen Profile. It is
contract-relative irreducibility, not proof that reality contains no missing stakeholder.

### What should happen when source coverage is incomplete?

**Answer: preserve Unknown and guard the plan.** Missing or candidate dependencies cannot prove either
impact or non-impact. A guarded discovery/evidence obligation is preferable to confident omission.

### Does every admitted dependency propagate every status change?

**Answer: no.** Edge existence states a business relationship, not a universal transfer function. The
Source Contract must declare a Profile-owned transfer predicate. Otherwise a healthy recovery would
spuriously activate every downstream domain, and the compiler would be smuggling business policy into
code. Unknown predicate inputs preserve Unknown; they do not default to true or false.

### Should the old OrgRebase folders be reorganized now?

**Answer: no.** They are a working product/reference baseline under a different license, not disposable
duplication. OAC is isolated in a clean repository. Only confirmed metadata debris is eligible for a
recoverable move to system Trash.

### What makes this “next generation” instead of a cleaner workflow?

**Answer:** organizational topology becomes replaceable output while enterprise obligations remain the
portable invariant. The leap is not more autonomy; it is **authority before autonomy**: generated
organizations can vary, yet every accepted variant must explain coverage against admitted facts and
survive an independent adversarial check.

### When is the MVP complete?

**Answer:** only when all of these are true:

1. schemas and detached digests are drift-checked;
2. the Supplier Profile derives typed, sourced obligations with explicit Unknown;
3. the compiler produces a minimal zero-effect plan;
4. the verifier accepts plural witnesses without compiler imports;
5. registered mutations meet the rejection threshold;
6. public-data cases and claims are provenance-correct;
7. clean local checks, coverage, packaging, and deterministic replay pass.

A demo that omits any item is progress, not completion.

## Consequence

Specs for runtime adapters, OutcomeCertificate effects, automatic learning, UI, broad enterprise
connectors, formal C2 claims, and cross-enterprise superiority remain design inputs. They do not enter
the first implementation merely because they are already written.
