## Keep evidence levels separate

- Same-run receipts establish recorded tasks, approval and outcomes, not paying customers.
- Public historical transactions support arithmetic checks; controlled discounts and taxes are not historical policies.
- OWB is a constructed comparison over a parallel reference SUT, not an empirical comparison of real single- and multi-Agent LLM products.
- Static source, installation checks and local runs do not qualify production capacity, customer IAM or connectors.

Consult the original-language [third-party inventory](../THIRD-PARTY-INVENTORY.md) and [historical/current build boundaries](../HISTORICAL-BUILD-VERIFICATION.md). Reports should retain actual run identities, failed and incomplete counts, and the applicable terms for each material.
